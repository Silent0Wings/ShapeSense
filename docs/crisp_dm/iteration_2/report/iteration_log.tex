Data Understanding & Measured: orientation carries class information in some sources. reevald squares and rectangles are 92 to 95\% axis-aligned, Quick, Draw! squares 73 to 85\%, El Korchi squares are randomly oriented. A0 is a template matcher: its accuracy on rotated validation falls from 93.65 at 0° to 60.9 at 45°, while A1 stays at 89 to 91. The source also gives the class away (source classifier 91.7\% against 46.7\%). \\
Data Preparation & Measured on validation, three seeds, not on test: A1 with rotation off reached 93.15 macro-F1 and with ±15° reached 93.17, against 91.69 for A0 and 90.03 for A1 with ±180°. Every augmentation and generator range had been set by hand. Two real defects found and fixed: augmented shapes came out about 6 px too small (51 px against 57 px real), and 1-px strokes broke into pieces after warping. The synthetic stroke was 2 px against 3.1 px real. No bug was found in labels or batching. \\
Modeling & Tuning covered only learning rate and dropout, on A1, and A0 reused A1's winner. A1 is still improving when the 30-epoch budget ends (60 epochs reached 92.11 and 92.38, against 90.44 and 90.71). Early stopping is fragile at learning rate 0.003: validation scores can swing by 30 points between epochs. The loss, polarity and class weighting were checked and are correct. After tuning on validation, mean macro-F1 was A2 95.17, A1 94.77, B 94.20, A0 92.61. Trials per arm: A0 6, A1 30, B 22, A2 18. \\

On unseen Hand-Drawn triangles and rectangles, mean accuracy was A0 66.6, A1 74.5, B 72.6, A2 73.8. \\
