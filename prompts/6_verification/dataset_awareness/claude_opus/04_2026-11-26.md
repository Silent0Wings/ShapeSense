**Quick, Draw! dataset.** I know this one well.

- **Creator:** Google Creative Lab. The drawings came from players of the online game *Quick, Draw!*, where people had about 20 seconds to sketch a prompted word.

- **Formats:**

  - **Simplified drawing files** (`.ndjson`). Drawings are aligned to the top-left, scaled to a 0–255 range, resampled at 1-pixel spacing, simplified with Ramer–Douglas–Peucker (epsilon 2.0), and timing is removed.
  - **Binary files** (`.bin`), a compact encoding of the simplified data.
  - **Numpy bitmaps** (`.npy`). These are **28×28 grayscale** rasterizations of the simplified drawings.
  - Sketch-RNN `.npz` files with train, validation and test splits.
- **Metadata fields:** `key_id`, `word`, `countrycode`, `timestamp`, `recognized`, `drawing`.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
